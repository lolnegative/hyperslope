Privacy

Hyperslope doesn't collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, controls, comfort options), your local high scores, your diamond and milestone progress, and your equipped cosmetics. They are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any developer server.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Endless and Daily Run scores and achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer does not receive or store your personal data.

Purchases

Optional cosmetic purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Hyperslope only checks your App Store entitlements on-device to unlock items you own.

What there isn't

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party servers, and no data shared with anyone.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, Hyperslope is safe for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page and the App Store listing will be updated first.
